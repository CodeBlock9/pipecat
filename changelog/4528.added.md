- `LLMService.collect_inference_usage()` returns the token usage accumulated by out-of-band `run_inference()` calls, and `record_inference_usage()` lets a service report it. `run_inference()` runs outside the pipeline, so its usage cannot travel the metrics-frame route — `start_llm_usage_metrics` is gated on a `StartFrame` a one-shot service never receives — and until now a summarization or extraction call was invisible to any usage accounting. The OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and AWS Bedrock services now record it.
