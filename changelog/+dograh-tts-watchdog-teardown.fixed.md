- Every way a synthesis can end now disarms its context watchdog, not only the arrival of audio. An interruption ends all of them (`_end_all_synthesis_watchdogs`), a context torn down by `_audio_context_task_handler` — which deletes rather than calling `remove_audio_context` — ends its own, and a generator that yielded an `ErrorFrame` arms none, since its failure has already been reported. Without these, arming after `run_tts` returns left a barge-in during the first sentence of a turn, a completed silent context, and every HTTP synthesis failure each firing a false timeout against `synthesis_timeout_burst`.
