- The per-context synthesis watchdog is disarmed as soon as a synthesis's shape is known, so a service that yields its own audio is bounded once rather than twice: by the first `TTSAudioRawFrame`, by the generator ending having yielded anything, and by the context being removed. A synthesis that legitimately produces no audio no longer fires a timeout after it is over, and a watchdog that has fired no longer leaves its entry in the map.
