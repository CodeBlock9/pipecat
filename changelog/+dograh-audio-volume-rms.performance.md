- `calculate_audio_volume` computes root-mean-square level on the ITU-R BS.1770 absolute scale instead of running a single-block EBU R128 integrated-loudness measurement through pyloudnorm. The scale is unchanged -- the BS.1770 offset and the -20..80 normalisation are both kept -- so a threshold calibrated against the old measurement, the VAD's `min_volume` above all, still means what it meant. It also takes pyloudnorm, and with it scipy, out of the import graph.
