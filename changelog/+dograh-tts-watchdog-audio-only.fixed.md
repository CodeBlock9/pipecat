- The per-context synthesis watchdog now decides "did this generator deliver the audio?" on a `TTSAudioRawFrame` and not on any yielded frame. Dograh, ElevenLabs, Rime and NVIDIA all yield a `TTSStartedFrame` from `run_tts` when the audio context does not exist yet — the first sentence of every turn — and then return with the audio still to arrive on their receive loop. Counting that control frame as delivery left the first sentence of every turn on those services with no synthesis deadline at all.
