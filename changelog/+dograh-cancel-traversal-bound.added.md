- `PipelineWorker.cancel()` accepts `cancel_timeout_secs`, bounding how long that one cancellation waits for its `CancelFrame` to reach the end of the pipeline before `on_pipeline_finished` fires. `None`, the default, keeps the worker's own `cancel_timeout_secs`. A caller that already knows the pipeline cannot forward frames — an output transport that has given up on its socket — passes a short value rather than holding the worker open for a frame that cannot arrive.
