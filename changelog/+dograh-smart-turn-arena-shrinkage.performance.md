- The local smart-turn analyzer asks ONNX Runtime to shrink its CPU arena after every inference, through a shared `RunOptions` carrying `memory.enable_memory_arena_shrinkage`. The arena is a high-water-mark pool sized by peak simultaneous inference and never returned on its own, so whatever a moment of overlap grew it to stayed resident for the life of the process. The session and its options are unchanged: the pool still serves the run that needs it.
