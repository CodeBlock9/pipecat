- The OpenAI chat-completions, Responses and Gemini request builders send `settings.extra` again, which they had stopped doing when provider options arrived, so a service configured with `Settings(extra=...)` — GPT-5's `verbosity`, DeepSeek's `thinking` — reaches the provider. `extra` is deep-merged first and the applied provider options over it: an applied option still wins, and a partial nested one such as `stream_options: {"include_obfuscation": false}` keeps the builder's `include_usage`. `DeepSeekLLMService` loses a private copy of the builder that nothing called; it uses the base builder, with its provider-options merge and `extra_body` routing.
