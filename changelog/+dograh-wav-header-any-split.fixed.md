- `_stream_audio_frames_from_iterator(strip_wav_header=True)` finds the WAV header however the stream delivers it: split across chunks, a byte at a time, or longer than 44 bytes because a chunk such as `LIST` comes before `data`, with the sample rate read from `fmt ` in every case. A raw stream is decided once, so a later chunk that begins with `RIFF` keeps its audio; a stream that ends inside its header logs a warning; and the tail frame carries the context id.
