- `VonageFrameSerializer` and `VobizFrameSerializer` bound the REST request they make to hang up a call at `CARRIER_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS` (5 s, in `pipecat.serializers.call_strategies`). The request runs inside the terminal frame's traversal, and under aiohttp's default total of 300 s a carrier that stopped answering held the pipeline's end for five minutes. A request that times out is logged like any other failed hangup, and the end of the call carries on.
