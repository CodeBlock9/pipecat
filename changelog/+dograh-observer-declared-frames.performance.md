- An observer can name the frame types its `on_push_frame` acts on in `BaseObserver.observed_frame_types`, and `WorkerObserver` then does not queue anything else for it. Audio is nearly all of what an observer sees on a telephony call and no observer in the tree acts on any of it, so every in-tree observer now declares; the default stays unfiltered, so an observer written against an older version keeps receiving every frame. Declarations follow `isinstance` semantics and are held against the handlers themselves by `tests/test_observer_declarations.py`.
