- `OpenAIRealtimeLLMService`'s manual-mode pre-roll (turn detection disabled) replays the audio that went on the wire. PCM input at another rate is resampled to 24 kHz before it is appended, but the pre-roll buffered the source audio and sized its window at the source rate, so after an interruption it replayed 8 or 16 kHz audio into a 24 kHz input buffer: a shorter, higher-pitched speech onset. It now buffers the resampled bytes and measures its window at the rate they were sent.
